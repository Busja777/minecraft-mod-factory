## Replant Click 1.2

Area harvest with a hoe.

- Right-click a ripe crop while holding a hoe: the ripe crops of the same kind in the 3×3 area around it (same height) are harvested and replanted too, each one exactly like a single click.
- Works for wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots and nether wart. Cocoa pods stay one at a time, because they hang on the sides of logs and not in a flat field.
- Each crop around the clicked one costs the hoe one point of durability (Unbreaking helps as usual); the clicked crop stays free. A hoe with its last point left stops the area and never breaks.
- Fortune on the hoe counts for every crop of the area. Young crops and crops of another kind are left alone.
- Crops around the clicked one that you may not change are skipped: spawn protection, the world border, and land protected by claim mods that block breaking there.
- New file `config/replantclick.json`, created on the first start: `areaWithHoe` (default `true`) turns the area off when set to `false`. A broken value falls back to the default with a warning in the server log.
- Without a hoe nothing changes. Sneaking and bone meal work as before, and only the server needs the mod.
