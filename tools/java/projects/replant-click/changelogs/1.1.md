## Replant Click 1.1

Now also for nether wart and cocoa pods.

- Right-click fully grown nether wart to harvest it; one nether wart from the drops is planted again, and Fortune still gives more.
- Right-click a ripe cocoa pod to pick it; one cocoa bean is planted again on the same side of the log.
- Young nether wart and young pods are left alone. Sneaking and bone meal work as before.
- Everything from 1.0 stays as it was. Only the server needs the mod.
