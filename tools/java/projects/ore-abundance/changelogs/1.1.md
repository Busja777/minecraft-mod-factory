## Ore Abundance 1.1

The Nether update.

- Nether quartz and nether gold ore are now about 1.5 times as common, by the same rule as the Overworld ores.
- Vein attempts per chunk: Nether quartz 16 → 24 (basalt deltas 32 → 48), nether gold ore 10 → 15 (basalt deltas 20 → 30).
- Ancient debris stays vanilla on purpose: it is too valuable to multiply.
- Vein sizes and heights stay vanilla; all Overworld changes of 1.0 stay as they were.
- Only Nether land generated after the update gets the extra ores; existing chunks do not change.
