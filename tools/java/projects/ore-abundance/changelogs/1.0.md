## Ore Abundance 1.0

First release.

- About 1.5 times as many ore veins in the Overworld: coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis lazuli, diamond and emerald.
- Vein attempts per chunk: coal 30 → 45 and 20 → 30, copper 16 → 24 (also the large veins of dripstone caves), iron 90 → 135, 10 → 15 and 10 → 15, gold 4 → 6 (badlands 50 → 75), redstone 4 → 6 and 8 → 12, lapis 2 → 3 and 4 → 6, diamond 7 → 10, 4 → 6 and 2 → 3, emerald 100 → 150. Large diamond veins: one in 6 chunks instead of one in 9.
- Vein sizes, heights and biomes stay vanilla; Nether ores do not change.
- Only land generated after the pack is added gets the extra ores; existing chunks do not change.
