## Ore Abundance 1.2

Amethyst geodes.

- Amethyst geodes are now about 1.5 times as common, by the same rule as the ores: one geode attempt every 16 chunks instead of every 24.
- Geode size, height (from 6 blocks above the bottom up to y 30) and contents stay vanilla, so budding amethyst stays as rare inside each geode as before.
- All ore changes of 1.0 and 1.1 stay as they were.
- Only land generated after the update gets the extra geodes; existing chunks do not change.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
