## Enchanting Extras 1.1

Three more enchantments.

- Quickstrike I–II for swords and axes: your attacks recharge 10% faster per level.
- Outreach for swords and axes: hit mobs and players from half a block further away. Quickstrike and Outreach exclude each other.
- Tempered I–II for helmets: one point of armour toughness per level, so big hits hurt a little less. Works together with any protection enchantment.
- Like the first six, they only come from the enchanting table, not from loot, villagers or mobs.
- On Minecraft 26.3, Quickstrike and Outreach also go on spears.
- Everything from 1.0 stays as it was. Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
