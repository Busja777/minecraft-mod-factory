## Hush 1.4

Wolves, horses and pistons.

- Wolves bark, pant and whine at 35 %. On 26.3 this covers every wolf sound variant (angry, big, cute, grumpy, puglin and sad wolves). A growling wolf stays at full volume, so you still hear when one is angry.
- Horses (calls and breathing), donkeys and mules play their calls at 35 %. Eating, steps, taming, hurt and death sounds are not touched.
- Pistons extend and retract at 40 %, so redstone machines are easier to live next to.
- Everything from 1.3 keeps its volume. No sound is muted and every sound keeps its subtitle.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
