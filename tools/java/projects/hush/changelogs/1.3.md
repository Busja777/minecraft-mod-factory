## Hush 1.3

The new pig, cow and chicken sounds of Minecraft 26.3 are quieter too.

- On 26.3, big and mini pigs, moody cows and picky chickens play their calls at 35 %, like the classic farm animals.
- Piglets and chicks have their own calls in 26.3; they play at 35 % as well.
- Nothing changes on 1.21 and 1.21.1: those versions do not have these sounds, and every sound from 1.2 keeps its volume.
- No sound is muted, hurt and death sounds are not touched, and every sound keeps its subtitle.
