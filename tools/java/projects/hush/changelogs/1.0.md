## Hush 1.0

First release.

- Villagers and wandering traders (idle, yes, no and trading sounds) play at 35 % of their volume, and so do cows, sheep, chickens and pigs.
- Nether portal hum, the portal whoosh and the travel sound play at 25 %.
- Rain plays at 45 %.
- Minecart rolling (outside, inside and underwater) plays at 35 %, anvil use, landing and placing at 40 %.
- No sound is muted, hurt and death sounds are not touched, and every sound keeps its subtitle.
