## Hush 1.2

More everyday sounds are quieter.

- Villager workstation sounds (all 13 professions, including the loud librarian lectern) play at 35 %.
- Goats (bleating and screaming goats) and chickens laying eggs play at 35 %, like the other farm animals.
- Bat squeaks and the bat take-off flutter play at 35 %.
- Bee buzzing and busy beehives play at 40 %; angry bees keep their full warning buzz.
- Furnace, smoker and blast furnace crackling play at 40 %.
- Everything from 1.1 stays as it was. No sound is muted and every sound keeps its subtitle.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
