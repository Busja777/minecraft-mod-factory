## Uncraft Basics 1.0

First release.

- Take wool of all 16 colours apart into 4 string.
- Take quartz blocks, quartz pillars and chiseled quartz blocks apart into 4 nether quartz each.
- Take clay, snow blocks, glowstone, amethyst blocks, bricks, nether bricks, honeycomb blocks and dripstone blocks apart into the 4 pieces they are made of.
- Take blue ice apart into 9 packed ice and packed ice into 9 ice.
- 29 shapeless crafting recipes; every amount matches the vanilla recipe that makes the block, so nothing is ever gained.
