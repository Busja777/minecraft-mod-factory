## Uncraft Basics 1.3

Copper, at every stage.

- 4 cut copper, 4 cut copper stairs, 4 chiseled copper or 4 copper grates give back 1 copper block, and 8 cut copper slabs give 1 block, as much as the stonecutter takes for them.
- 4 copper bulbs give back 3 copper blocks (the blaze rod and redstone are used up).
- The stage stays the same: exposed cut copper turns into an exposed copper block, oxidized grates into an oxidized block, and waxed blocks give waxed blocks.
- Copper doors give 2 copper ingots and copper trapdoors 3 (4 on 26.3, where a trapdoor takes 4 ingots), at every stage, waxed or not.
- Exposed, weathered and oxidized copper blocks (also waxed) give 9 copper ingots, like the plain copper block already does.
- Lightning rods give 3 copper ingots.
- 71 new recipes, 126 in total. Every amount is what the cheapest vanilla recipe takes, so you never get more copper or wax back than went in.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
