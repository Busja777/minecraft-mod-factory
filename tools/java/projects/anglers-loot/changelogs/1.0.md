## Anglers Loot 1.0

First release.

- About half as much junk when fishing: the junk weight goes from 10 to 5.
- Treasure about one and a half times as often: the treasure weight goes from 5 to 8, still only in open water.
- Two new treasure catches: the heart of the sea (rare) and one to three bottles o' enchanting.
- The vanilla treasures stay about as likely per cast as before, and Luck of the Sea still lowers junk and raises treasure; at level III no junk is caught at all.
