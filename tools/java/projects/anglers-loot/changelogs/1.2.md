## Anglers Loot 1.2

Biome catches: the fish you catch now depend on where you fish.

- Warm oceans, jungles and mangrove swamps: tropical fish make up about one in five fish (vanilla: one in fifty).
- Frozen rivers, frozen oceans and taigas: salmon make up two in five fish (vanilla: one in four).
- Cold oceans: cod make up about two in three fish, a little more than elsewhere.
- Everywhere else the fish are as in vanilla. Junk, treasure and fish stay exactly as likely as in 1.1, in every biome; only which fish you get changes.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3, like before.
