## Anglers Loot 1.3

A new advancement tab, Angler's Logbook, with 13 advancements about fishing.

- Catch each fish with a rod: cod, salmon, tropical fish and pufferfish.
- Biome catches: a tropical fish in a warm ocean, jungle or mangrove swamp, a salmon in a frozen river, frozen ocean or taiga, and a cod in a cold ocean.
- Treasure: fish up any treasure, an enchanted book, a bottle o' enchanting and the rare heart of the sea.
- Two challenges: Full Creel (catch all four fish) and Sunken Hoard (fish up every treasure).
- Only real catches count, not reeling in mobs or dropped items. Some advancements give experience, none give items.
- The fishing loot stays exactly as in 1.2. Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3 as before.
