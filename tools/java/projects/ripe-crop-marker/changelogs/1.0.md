## Ripe Crop Marker 1.0

First release.

- Fully grown wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots and nether wart show a small golden sparkle just above their leaves, so you see at a glance which crops are ready to harvest.
- Only the last growth stage changes: younger crops look exactly as in vanilla, and the plant itself keeps every pixel of the vanilla texture.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
