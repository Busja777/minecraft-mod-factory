## Ripe Crop Marker 1.0

First release.

- Fully grown wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots and nether wart show a small sparkle at the top of the plant.
- The sparkle is five pixels: a warm white centre with four pale gold arms, the same on every crop.
- Only the last growth stage changes; every earlier stage, the items and the drops look and work exactly as before.
- Works on Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
