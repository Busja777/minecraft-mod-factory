## Shield Down Low 1.0

First release.

- In first person, the shield sits 4 pixels lower in either hand, so it covers less of the screen.
- Raising it to block moves it up and turns it just like before, only from a lower spot: you still clearly see when you are blocking.
- Third person, the inventory, item frames and the ground show the shield as usual (on 26.3 shelves too).
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
