## Shield Down Low 1.0

First release.

- The shield in first person sits 3/16 of a block lower and 2/16 further out to the side when held, in either hand.
- While blocking, the raised shield sits 4/16 of a block lower and 2/16 further out, so the middle of the screen stays clear.
- It still rises when you block, and its angle and size are unchanged.
- Third person, the inventory, item frames and dropped shields look exactly as before.
- Works on Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
