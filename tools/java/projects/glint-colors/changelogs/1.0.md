## Glint Colors 1.0

First release.

- The enchantment glint becomes a soft rainbow of 8 colours: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, violet and pink.
- The colours drift over enchanted items as the glint moves, one full round about every 7.5 seconds at the default glint speed.
- Works on items, books, armour, elytra, tridents and shields, in your hand, in the inventory and on the ground.
- The shimmer keeps the vanilla streak pattern and about the same brightness, so it still looks enchanted.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
