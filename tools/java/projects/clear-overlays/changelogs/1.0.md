## Clear Overlays 1.0

First release.

- The carved pumpkin view keeps its face shape, but its dark part now covers only 40 percent of the screen, so you can still see where you walk.
- The powder snow frost keeps its crystals at the screen edges at half strength, and it still fades in while you freeze.
- Built from the vanilla textures, pixel for pixel: only how strongly they cover the world changes.
- Works on Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
