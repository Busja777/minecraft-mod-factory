## Villages Nearby 1.2

Outposts and temples are closer too.

- Pillager outposts, desert pyramids, jungle temples, swamp huts and igloos spawn about 2.3 times as often: one attempt every 21 chunks instead of every 32 (at least 6 chunks apart instead of 8).
- Villages stay as close together as in 1.1.
- Outposts still keep their distance from villages and stay as rare per attempt as in vanilla; every structure still only appears in its usual biomes.
- Only land generated after the update gets the extra structures; existing chunks do not change.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
