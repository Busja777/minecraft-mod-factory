## Villages Nearby 1.3

Ocean ruins and shipwrecks are closer too.

- Ocean ruins spawn about 2.4 times as often: one attempt every 13 chunks instead of every 20 (at least 6 chunks apart instead of 8).
- Shipwrecks, sunken and beached, spawn about 2.2 times as often: one attempt every 16 chunks instead of every 24 (at least 3 chunks apart instead of 4).
- Ocean monuments stay as rare as in vanilla: with their gold blocks, sponges and the Mining Fatigue of the elder guardians, more of them would make the oceans both richer and harder to explore than intended.
- Villages, outposts and temples stay as close together as in 1.2; every structure still only appears in its usual biomes.
- Only land generated after the update gets the extra structures; existing chunks do not change.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
