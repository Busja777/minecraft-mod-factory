## Villages Nearby 1.0

First release.

- Villages spawn about 2.4 times as often: one village attempt every 22 chunks instead of every 34 (at least 6 chunks apart instead of 8).
- Plains, desert, savanna, snowy and taiga villages keep their vanilla chances and still only appear in their usual biomes.
- Only land generated after the pack is added gets the extra villages; existing chunks do not change.
