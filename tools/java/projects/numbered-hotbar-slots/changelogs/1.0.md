## Numbered Hotbar Slots 1.0

First release.

- Each of the nine hotbar slots shows its number (1 to 9) in its top-left corner.
- The digits are 3 × 5 pixels, light grey with a dark shadow, drawn on the vanilla hotbar texture.
- Items, stack counts and durability bars are drawn on top, and the selection frame is unchanged.
- Works on Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
