## Owner Safe Pets 1.2

Your own potions spare your pets.

- Splash and lingering potions you throw no longer give your tamed animals harmful effects: no poison, weakness, slowness or any other harmful effect. This also covers the cloud a lingering potion leaves behind. (Harming potions already did no damage to your pets.)
- Helpful effects still work: a healing or regeneration potion you throw at your pet heals it as before. A mixed potion such as turtle master gives your pet the resistance but not the slowness.
- Your pet no longer uses up your own harmful lingering cloud while it stands in it, so the cloud lasts as long as it would without the pet.
- Potions thrown by other players, witches or dispensers are unchanged.
- New setting `protectFromOwnerPotions` in `config/ownersafepets.json` (default `true`). Set it to `false` and your potions affect your pets as in vanilla. Config files from 1.1 keep working; without the setting the default is used. `sneakToHit` still only concerns your own hits.
- Everything from 1.1 stays the same.
