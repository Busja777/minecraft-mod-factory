## Owner Safe Pets 1.0

First release.

- Tamed wolves, cats, parrots, horses, donkeys, mules, llamas and camels take no damage from the player who tamed them.
- This covers direct hits, sword sweeps, arrows, tridents and other projectiles you shot, and explosions you caused, such as TNT you lit.
- A sitting wolf or parrot stays seated when you hit it by mistake.
- Damage from anyone or anything else is unchanged.
- Only the server needs the mod: players without it are covered too.
