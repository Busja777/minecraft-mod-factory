## Fast Leaf Fall 1.0

First release.

- When a tree loses its logs, its leaves decay within about three seconds instead of several minutes.
- The decay is the game's own: same drops (saplings, sticks, apples), same rule (no log within six blocks).
- Leaves placed by players never decay, and leaves that still reach another trunk stay.
- Works however the logs disappear, by axe, fire or explosion.
- Only the server needs the mod: players without it see the leaves fall too.
