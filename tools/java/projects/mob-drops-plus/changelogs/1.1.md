## Mob Drops Plus 1.1

Ten more mobs, now twenty in all.

- Piglin: 0–2 arrows, like the pillager. Still no gold: bartering stays the way to get it.
- Hoglin: 0–1 crimson fungus. Strider: 0–1 warped fungus (what each is bred with).
- Polar bear: 0–1 leather. Glow squid: 0–1 glow lichen. Breeze: 0–1 wind charge.
- Spider: a cobweb, like the cave spider. Witch: a nether wart. Frog: a slime ball. Evoker: a book. Each only when a player kills the mob, 5% of the time (up to 11% with Looting III).
- Looting adds up to one more per level to every amount above. The ten mobs of 1.0 and all vanilla drops stay as they were.
