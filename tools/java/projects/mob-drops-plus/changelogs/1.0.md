## Mob Drops Plus 1.0

First release.

- One small extra drop for ten mobs, for Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
- Husk: 0–2 sand. Drowned: 0–1 kelp. Pillager: 0–2 arrows.
- Zoglin and goat: 0–1 leather. Ravager and camel: 0–2 leather.
- Fox: 0–1 sweet berries. Ocelot: 0–2 string, like the cat.
- Cave spider: a cobweb, 5% of the time when a player kills it (up to 11% with Looting III).
- Looting adds up to one more per level to every amount above. All vanilla drops stay as they were.
