## More Trim Materials 1.0

First release.

- Eight new armor trim materials for the smithing table: coal, obsidian, echo shard, slime ball, breeze rod, nether brick, popped chorus fruit and prismarine shard.
- Each material has its own color palette, shown on worn armor and on the armor icon, and its name in the tooltip.
- Works with all 18 trim patterns on leather, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond and netherite armor and the turtle shell. Copper armor is not included yet.
- One zip for both sides: put it into the world's datapacks folder and into resourcepacks.
