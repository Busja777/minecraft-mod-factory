## Trigger Homes 1.0

First release.

- `/trigger sethome` saves the block you stand on as your home (one home per player, Overworld, Nether or End).
- `/trigger home` teleports you to the middle of that block, also from another dimension.
- `/trigger spawn` teleports you to the world spawn.
- Home and spawn share a 30-second cooldown; the message tells you how many seconds are left.
- Works for every player without operator rights.
