## Trigger Homes 1.2

Back to where you died.

- New: `/trigger back` takes you to the place of your last death, so you can pick up your things. Like the other commands, it needs no operator rights.
- It works once per death: after you used it, it waits until you die again. Deaths from before you installed 1.2 do not count.
- Works for deaths in the Overworld, the Nether and the End, also from another dimension.
- It refuses to take you into lava or into the void and tells you why. Nothing is used up then.
- `/trigger back` shares the 30-second cooldown with `/trigger home` and `/trigger spawn`.
- Your homes from 1.0 and 1.1 are kept.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
