## Arrow Blocks 1.0

First release.

- Hoppers that feed sideways show an arrow on the floor of their bowl, pointing at the spout; hoppers that drop straight down show a small ring above their spout.
- Droppers and dispensers show arrows on the top, the bottom and both sides, pointing towards their front.
- Observers get arrows on both sides that point to their output, matching the vanilla arrow on top.
- The arrows turn with the block in every direction; furnaces keep their vanilla look.
- Works on Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
