## Arrow Blocks 1.0

First release.

- Hoppers show where their items go: arrows on the sides of the funnel (all four on a hopper that points down, the two sides of a sideways one) and one on the floor inside a sideways hopper.
- Dispensers and droppers show arrows on the four sides around the front, pointing at the front where items come out.
- Observers show arrows on their two plain sides, pointing to the back where the signal goes out, the same way the triangle on top already does.
- The arrows turn with the block in every direction. Fronts, backs and everything else keep their vanilla look, and the items in your inventory stay as they are.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
