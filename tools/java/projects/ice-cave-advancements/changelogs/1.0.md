## Ice Cave Advancements 1.0

First release, for Minecraft 26.4.

- A new advancement tab, Frozen Depths, with 18 advancements about the ice caves.
- Exploring: the ice caves, icicles, ice crystals, brittle ice and the music disc from igloos.
- Fighting the cold: the Frostbite, its ice balls, the Freezing effect and the Potion of Freezing.
- Building: packed ice bricks, chiseled packed ice, snow bricks, polished calcite, calcite bricks and the calcite pillar.
- Three challenges with experience as the reward. None give items.
