## Actionbar Coords 1.0

First release.

- Shows your block coordinates (X, Y, Z) and the time of day (24-hour clock) above the hotbar, four times a second.
- On for every player by default; `/trigger coords` hides the line for you and shows it again.
- The clock follows the Overworld's day, also in the Nether and the End.
- Works for every player without operator rights; one file for Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
