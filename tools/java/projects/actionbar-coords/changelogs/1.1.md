## Actionbar Coords 1.1

Now shows the direction you are facing.

- The line shows where you look between the coordinates and the clock: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W or NW (the same directions as the F3 screen), for example `X 120  Y 64  Z -33 · NE · 14:05`.
- Still four updates a second, and `/trigger coords` still hides and shows the line for you.
- Everything from 1.0 stays as it was. Works for every player without operator rights, on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
