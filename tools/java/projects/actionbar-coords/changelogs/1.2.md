## Actionbar Coords 1.2

Colour styles.

- New: six colour styles for the line above the hotbar: Classic, Plain, Gold, Green, Blue and Quiet (all grey, for players who want it less visible).
- `/trigger coords set 2` switches to the next style and tells you its name. Each player picks their own; no operator rights needed.
- `/trigger coords` still shows or hides the line, as before.
- Everyone starts with Classic, the colours of 1.0 and 1.1, so nothing changes unless you pick another style.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3.
