## Fresh Splashes 1.0

First release.

- The yellow splash text on the title screen now picks from 300 new, original lines instead of the vanilla list.
- The lines are about mining, farming, mobs, villages, the Nether, the End, redstone, building and more, all short enough to read at a glance.
- The special date splashes built into the game (Christmas, New Year, Halloween) still appear on their days.
