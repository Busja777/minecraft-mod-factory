## Bottled Levels 1.0

First release.

- Sneak and right-click an enchanting table with a glass bottle to fill it with 50 of your experience points. The bottle holds 45 of them: 10% is lost on bottling.
- The filled bottle is a Bottle o' Enchanting named "Bottled Experience" that shows the amount it holds. Thrown by hand or shot from a dispenser, it releases exactly that amount as orbs. Plain bottles still give their usual 3 to 11.
- With fewer than 50 points nothing happens and a short hint shows above the hotbar. Levels go down as needed; the exact points are kept.
- Filled bottles stack with each other but not with plain ones.
- Only the server needs the mod: players without it fill and throw bottles the same way.
