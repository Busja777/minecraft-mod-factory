## Stonecutter Woodworks 1.0

First release.

- Cut planks into stairs, slabs, fences, fence gates, doors, trapdoors and pressure plates in the stonecutter.
- Works for oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove and cherry planks: 56 recipes.
- One plank gives two slabs or one of everything else, so the amounts stay close to the crafting table.
