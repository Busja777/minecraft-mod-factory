## Stonecutter Woodworks 1.2

Three more woods.

- Bamboo, crimson and warped planks now work in the stonecutter too: stairs, slabs, fences, fence gates, doors, trapdoors and pressure plates.
- 21 new recipes, 77 in total, with the same amounts as before: one plank gives two slabs or one of everything else.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3 as before. Pale oak is not included, because it only exists in 26.3.
