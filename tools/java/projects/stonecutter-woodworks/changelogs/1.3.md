## Stonecutter Woodworks 1.3

Logs and bamboo mosaic.

- Logs, wood, stems, hyphae and blocks of bamboo, stripped or not, now go into the stonecutter too: they give planks and every block the planks give, all from one slot.
- One log gives 4 planks (a block of bamboo 2), the same as the crafting table, and of every other block exactly as much as those planks give in the stonecutter, for example 8 slabs from one log. Cutting a log is never better than first cutting it into planks.
- Bamboo planks now also cut into bamboo mosaic (one plank, one mosaic, like two bamboo slabs at the crafting table), and bamboo mosaic into mosaic stairs and slabs.
- Logs are not cut into stripped logs, because an axe already strips them for free.
- 347 new recipes, 424 in total. The recipes from 1.2 stay the same.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3 as before. Pale oak is still not included, because it only exists in 26.3.
