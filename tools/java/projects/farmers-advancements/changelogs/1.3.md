## Farmers Advancements 1.3

15 new advancements about cooking.

- Roasts: eat a steak, a cooked porkchop, cooked chicken, cooked mutton and a cooked rabbit.
- Bowls and bottles: eat mushroom stew and suspicious stew, drink a bottle of honey.
- Cooking: take food out of a smoker and put raw food on a lit campfire.
- Baking and crafting: bake a cake and eat a slice of it, craft a golden carrot and a glistering melon slice.
- A new challenge, Farm to Table: eat every farm food once, from carrots and bread to cake, stews and all five roasts.
- The Farmer's Almanac tab now has 60 advancements. Some of the new ones give experience, none give items.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3 as before; the first 45 advancements are unchanged.
