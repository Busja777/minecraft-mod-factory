## Farmers Advancements 1.2

15 new advancements about animals.

- Breed mooshrooms, goats, bees, armadillos, horses, donkeys, llamas and camels.
- Tame a donkey and a llama.
- Animal care: feed wheat to a calf, shear a sheep, milk a goat and fill a bowl with mooshroom stew.
- A new challenge, Wide Open Range: breed all eight of the new animals.
- The Farmer's Almanac tab now has 45 advancements. Some of the new ones give experience, none give items.
- Works on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1 and 26.3 as before; the first 30 advancements are unchanged.
