## Farmers Advancements 1.0

First release.

- A new advancement tab, Farmer's Almanac, with 30 advancements about farming.
- Planting: wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, pumpkins, melons, sweet berries, nether wart, torchflowers and pitcher pods.
- Harvesting, animals, farm food and farm tools, from a full stack of wheat to breeding cows, sheep, pigs, chickens and rabbits.
- Three challenges: collect every crop, breed every farm animal and eat a full harvest feast.
- Some advancements give experience. None give items.
