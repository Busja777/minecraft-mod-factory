## Joja Junk Mail 1.0

First release.

- A JojaMart advertising letter arrives every Wednesday morning: sixteen different letters a year, four per season, on the Joja letter paper. From year 2 on, an anniversary letter replaces the welcome letter.
- The letters are just for fun: no items, no money, no effect on the game.
- They stop once the Community Center is restored or the Joja community development form is completed.
