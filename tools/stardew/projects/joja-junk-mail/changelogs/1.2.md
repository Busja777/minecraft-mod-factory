## Joja Junk Mail 1.2

Festival letters.

- JojaMart now writes before every festival too: eleven new letters a year, one shortly before each festival, from the Egg Festival to the Feast of the Winter Star, including the Trout Derby, SquidFest and the Night Market. In English and German.
- A festival letter arrives the day before the festival, or two days before when that day is a Wednesday, so there is never more than one Joja letter on the same morning.
- The Wednesday letters stay as they are, and all letters still stop once the Community Center is restored or the Joja community development form is completed.
