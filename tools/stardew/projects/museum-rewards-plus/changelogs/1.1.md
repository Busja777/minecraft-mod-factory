## Museum Rewards Plus 1.1

Letters from Gunther.

- New: the morning after you collect one of the ten new museum rewards, a short thank-you letter from Gunther waits in your mailbox. Ten different letters, one per reward, each sent only once.
- The letters only say thanks: the reward itself is still the one Gunther hands you at his desk.
- If you already collected some rewards with 1.0, the letters for them arrive the next morning.
- All rewards and milestones stay exactly as in 1.0; the original museum rewards are unchanged.
