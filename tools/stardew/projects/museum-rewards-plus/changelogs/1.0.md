## Museum Rewards Plus 1.0

First release.

- Ten new rewards from Gunther for donating to the museum, at milestones the game does not reward yet.
- Minerals: 6, 16, 26, 36 and 46 donated give geodes, frozen geodes, magma geodes, omni geodes and gold bars.
- Artifacts: 6, 10, 25 and 35 donated give clay, mixed seeds, a rare seed and an artifact trove.
- 10 minerals and 10 artifacts together give nine blueberry seeds.
- All original museum rewards stay exactly as they are.
