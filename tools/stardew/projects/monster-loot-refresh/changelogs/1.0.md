## Monster Loot Refresh 1.0

First release.

- Eleven common monsters of the Mines sometimes drop one or two extra items that fit their floor.
- Slimes pick up the ore of their floor: copper ore from green slimes, iron ore from blue slimes, gold ore from red slimes.
- Rock crabs drop stone and copper ore, duggies clay, grubs a cave carrot, dust sprites iron ore, metal heads gold ore.
- Stone golems, frost bats and lava crabs can drop the geode of their floor.
- Every vanilla drop keeps its chance. No bars, gems or rare items are added.
