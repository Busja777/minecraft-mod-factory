## Happy Barn 1.0

First release.

- Animals that take two days or more between produce make it one day sooner: ducks and goats every day, rabbits every 3 days, ostriches and dinosaurs every 6 days.
- Sheep grow their wool one day sooner from 1 heart on, instead of from 4.5 hearts.
- Choose the level in the config: Gentle, Generous (default) or Abundant.
- Chickens, cows and pigs stay as they are. Produce, quality, prices and feeding are unchanged.
