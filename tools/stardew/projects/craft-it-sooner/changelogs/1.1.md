## Craft It Sooner 1.1

Four more farm basics come sooner.

- Quality Fertilizer, Deluxe Speed-Gro, the Bee House and the Tapper now unlock earlier too, with the same config setting.
- At Medium (default): Quality Fertilizer at Farming 7 instead of 9, Deluxe Speed-Gro at 6 instead of 8, the Bee House at 2 instead of 3, and the Tapper at Foraging 2 instead of 4.
- Light: 8, 7, 2 and Foraging 3. Strong: 6, 5, 1 and Foraging 1.
- Your setting carries over. Ingredients and results stay as they are. The Keg and the Iridium Sprinkler keep their usual levels.
- If your level is already high enough, the game adds the new recipes when the save loads.
