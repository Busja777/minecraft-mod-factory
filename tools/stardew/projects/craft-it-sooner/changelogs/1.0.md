## Craft It Sooner 1.0

First release.

- The Sprinkler, Quality Sprinkler, Basic Retaining Soil, Quality Retaining Soil and Speed-Gro recipes unlock at a lower Farming level.
- Choose the level in the config: Light, Medium (default) or Strong. At Medium, the Sprinkler unlocks at Farming 1 instead of 2 and the Quality Sprinkler at 4 instead of 6.
- Ingredients and results stay as they are. The Iridium Sprinkler is unchanged.
