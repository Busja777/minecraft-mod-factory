## Generous Geodes 1.0

First release.

- Geodes, frozen geodes and magma geodes hold a mineral or their artifact more often, and plain stone, clay and ore less often.
- Choose the level in the config: Gentle (60 % minerals), Generous (70 %, default) or Lavish (80 %). The normal game gives 50 %.
- Every geode only gives what it gives in the normal game, so all minerals and artifacts stay findable for the museum.
