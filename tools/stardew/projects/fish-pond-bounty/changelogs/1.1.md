## Fish Pond Bounty 1.1

More fish.

- 7 more fish get the same roe boost: catfish, sunfish, rainbow trout, spook fish, dorado, lingcod and pike. That makes 30 fish in all.
- Their special finds stay as rare as in the normal game: solar essence from full sunfish ponds, rainbow shells and prismatic shards from rainbow trout, treasure chests from spook fish and bug meat from dorado, lingcod and pike.
- Quests and spawn times of the new fish stay as in the normal game. The 23 fish of 1.0 are unchanged, legendary fish too.
