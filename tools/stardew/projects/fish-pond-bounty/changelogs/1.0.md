## Fish Pond Bounty 1.0

First release.

- Ponds of 23 common fish give roe whenever they produce: bream, bullhead, carp, chub, goby, herring, largemouth and smallmouth bass, perch, salmon, shad, walleye, albacore, anchovy, eel, halibut, pufferfish, red mullet, red snapper, sardine, sea cucumber, tilapia and tuna.
- Ponds with 4 or more fish sometimes give 1-2 roe, ponds with 7 or more fish sometimes 2-3 roe.
- Quests, spawn times and the daily chance to produce stay as in the normal game. Legendary fish are not changed.
