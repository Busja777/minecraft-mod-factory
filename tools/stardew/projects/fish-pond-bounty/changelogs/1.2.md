## Fish Pond Bounty 1.2

Choose your boost.

- New config option "Boost" (config.json or the in-game config menu) with three levels:
  - Gentle: ponds with 7 or more fish sometimes give 1-2 roe.
  - Normal (default, the same as before): ponds with 4 or more fish sometimes give 1-2 roe, ponds with 7 or more fish sometimes 2-3.
  - Generous: the same steps, but more often. A pond with 7 or more fish then averages 1.9 roe each time it produces (Normal: 1.7, Gentle: 1.2).
- At every level, a pond whose daily roll succeeds always gives roe, and never less than in the normal game.
- Special finds, quests, spawn times and the daily chance to produce stay as in the normal game at every level. Legendary fish are not changed.
