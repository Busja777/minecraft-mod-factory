## Town Board Orders 1.0

First release.

- Six new special orders on the town board, all about farm life: cheese for Gus, jelly or pickles for Pierre, eggs for Shane, milk for Jodi, flowers for Evelyn and foraged goods for Leah.
- Each order pays money and one heart of friendship with the villager who asked. No item rewards.
- An order only appears once your farm makes what it asks for: cheese, preserves, eggs or milk. Evelyn's flowers wait until the winter is over.
- All six can come back after they are done, like the town's other repeatable orders.
