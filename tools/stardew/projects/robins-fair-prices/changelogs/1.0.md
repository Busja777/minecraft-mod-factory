## Robin's Fair Prices 1.0

First release.

- The farm buildings from the carpenter cost less gold and fewer materials: coops, barns, sheds and their upgrades, fish pond, mill, silo, slime hutch, stable, well, shipping bin and pet bowl.
- Choose the share of the normal cost in the config: 75 %, 50 % (default) or 25 %. At 50 %, a barn costs 3,000g, 175 wood and 75 stone instead of 6,000g, 350 wood and 150 stone.
- Construction is faster too: buildings that took two days are done the next day at 50 % and 25 %, a new barn or coop takes two days instead of three (one day at 25 %). Instant buildings stay instant.
- Magical buildings and house upgrades are unchanged.
