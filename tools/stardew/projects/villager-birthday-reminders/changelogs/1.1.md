## Villager Birthday Reminders 1.1

A reminder the day before.

- New: a short letter the morning before each birthday ("Tomorrow is …'s birthday!"), with the same gift idea, so there is a day to get the gift ready.
- On by default. Don't want it? Switch off "DayBefore" in the config (config.json or the in-game config menu); the letter on the birthday itself always comes.
- A birthday on the 1st gets its reminder on the 28th of the season before (Krobus: fall 28).
- As before, letters only come for villagers you have already met.
