## Fruitful Orchards 1.0

First release.

- Fruit trees can grow two fruits on a day instead of one: cherry, apricot, orange, peach, pomegranate, apple, banana and mango.
- Choose the chance of the second fruit in the config: 25 %, 50 % (default) or 100 %.
- Growth time stays at 28 days. Seasons, fruit quality and the trees' look stay as they are.
