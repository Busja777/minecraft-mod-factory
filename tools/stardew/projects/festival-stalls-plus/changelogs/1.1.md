## Festival Stalls Plus 1.1

The Night Market joins in.

- The magic shop boat at the Night Market now sells a little more each night, up to 10 of each per day:
- Night 1 (winter 15): Crystal Fruit (230g) and Coral (120g).
- Night 2 (winter 16): Snow Yam (150g) and Sea Urchin (240g).
- Night 3 (winter 17): Crocus (90g) and Nautilus Shell (180g).
- Same price rule as before: 1.5 times the sell price, so nothing can be sold back at a profit. The boat's own stock stays as it is.
- The Desert Festival is not changed: its shops take Calico Eggs, not gold. All booths from 1.0 stay exactly as they were.
