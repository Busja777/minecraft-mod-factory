## Festival Stalls Plus 1.2

Choose your prices.

- New config setting "Prices" for every good this pack adds to the booths: Fair (1.25 times the sell price), Normal (1.5 times, default) or Steep (2 times), rounded up to 10g.
- Normal keeps the prices of 1.0 and 1.1, so nothing changes unless you pick another level. Fair example: Egg 70g, Pumpkin 400g, Crystal Fruit 190g. Steep example: Egg 100g, Pumpkin 640g, Crystal Fruit 300g.
- Even at Fair nothing can be sold back at a profit, also not with the Rancher or Tiller profession.
- Seeds still cost what Pierre asks, at every level. Each booth's own stock, the daily limits and the festivals from 1.0 and 1.1 stay as they were.
