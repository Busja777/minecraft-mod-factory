## Stocked Shelves 1.2

Void Salmon on Wednesdays.

- Krobus now sells Void Salmon on Wednesdays only, 5 at a time, like the fish he sells himself on that day. Price and condition stay the same: 300g, after you have caught one.
- Why: a fish smoker turns a fish into smoked fish worth twice as much, and the Fisher, Angler and Artisan professions raise that further. With all of them, 5 Void Salmon every day turned into a steady profit, more than the game itself allows with Krobus' own Wednesday fish.
- Squid Ink and Bug Steak stay available every day, as before. Willy's shelves are unchanged.
