## Stocked Shelves 1.1

Krobus restocks too.

- Krobus' shop in the sewers also sells Void Salmon (300g), Squid Ink (220g) and Bug Steak (100g), up to 5 of each per day.
- Each one appears once you could get it yourself: Void Salmon after you have caught one, Squid Ink after you have caught a Squid or a Midnight Squid, Bug Steak once you know its recipe.
- Prices follow Krobus' own: twice the sell price, like his Void Essence and Bat Wings.
- His own stock stays as it is. No Void Mayonnaise, no Monster Musk, no Bug Meat. Willy's shelves from 1.0 are unchanged.
