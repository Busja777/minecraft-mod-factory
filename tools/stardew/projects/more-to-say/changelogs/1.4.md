## More to Say 1.4

Evelyn, George and the Wizard join in.

- Evelyn, George and the Wizard have 12 new lines each: one per season for meeting them, one for a rainy or snowy day (from 2 hearts) and one for a closer friendship (from 6 hearts).
- Evelyn talks about the town gardens, her seasonal cookies and George; George about his television, leeks and the cold; the Wizard about the elementals, the forest and the mysteries around us.
- Each line is said once, in their own voice, and only mentions what you already know at that friendship level.
- All 36 new lines are in English and German, like the rest of the pack.
- Linus, Willy, Pam, Gus, Marnie and Robin are unchanged: same lines, same timing.
