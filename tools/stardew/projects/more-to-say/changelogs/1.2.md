## More to Say 1.2

Pam and Gus join in.

- Pam and Gus have 12 new lines each: one per season for meeting them, one for a rainy or snowy day (from 2 hearts) and one for a closer friendship (from 6 hearts).
- Pam talks about parsnips, her stiff old legs and Penny; Gus about his kitchen, the seasons' best ingredients and the Saloon.
- Each line is said once, in their own voice, and only mentions what you already know at that friendship level.
- All 24 new lines are in English and German, like the rest of the pack.
- Linus and Willy are unchanged: same lines, same timing.
