## More to Say 1.0

First release.

- Linus and Willy have 12 new lines each: one per season for meeting them, one for a rainy or snowy day (from 2 hearts) and one for a closer friendship (from 6 hearts).
- Each line is said once, in their own voice, without replacing any of their usual dialogue.
- Lines only talk about things you already know at that friendship level.
