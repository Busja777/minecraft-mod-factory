## Bountiful Forage 1.0

First release.

- More forage can appear in Cindersap Forest and on the Beach: the forage limit of each map rises from 6 to 8, 9 (default) or 12, and more spawn attempts are made each night.
- Choose +25 %, +50 % (default) or +100 % in the config, with config texts in English and German.
- Only the items the base game already spawns there, in their own seasons. Tide pool items and spring onions stay unchanged.
