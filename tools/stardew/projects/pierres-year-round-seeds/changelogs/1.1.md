## Pierre's Year-Round Seeds 1.1

Sandy's Oasis shop joins in.

- Sandy sells her seasonal seeds all year: rhubarb seeds (spring), starfruit seeds (summer) and beet seeds (fall), also in winter.
- They use the same surcharge setting as Pierre's seeds: at +50 % (default) rhubarb seeds cost 150g instead of 100g, starfruit seeds 600g instead of 400g and beet seeds 30g instead of 20g.
- In their own season, Sandy's stock and prices stay as they are. Cactus seeds and everything else in her shop are untouched.
- Your surcharge setting carries over. Crops still only grow in their own season.
