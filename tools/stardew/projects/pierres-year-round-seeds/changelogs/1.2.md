## Pierre's Year-Round Seeds 1.2

The settings are now in German too.

- The surcharge setting has a readable name, description and value names in the in-game config menu (Generic Mod Config Menu): "Out-of-season surcharge" (+25 %, +50 %, +100 %) in English, "Aufpreis außerhalb der Saison" in German. Players with the game set to German see the setting in German; everyone else keeps the English one.
- Same seeds, same prices at Pierre's and Sandy's; your setting carries over and nothing else changes.
