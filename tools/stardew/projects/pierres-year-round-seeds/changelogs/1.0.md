## Pierre's Year-Round Seeds 1.0

First release.

- Pierre sells the spring, summer and fall seeds of his shop all year, also in winter.
- Out-of-season seeds cost more: choose +25 %, +50 % (default) or +100 % in the config.
- Seeds he only sells from year 2 on (garlic, rice, red cabbage, artichoke) still wait for year 2.
- Crops still only grow in their own season.
