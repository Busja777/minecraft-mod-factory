## Machine Tempo 1.0

First release.

- Kegs and preserves jars finish their goods faster: wine, juice, beer, pale ale, mead, coffee, green tea, vinegar, jelly, pickles, caviar and aged roe.
- Choose the speed in the config: 1.0 (normal), 1.25, 1.5 (default) or 2.0.
- Only the processing time changes. Inputs, outputs and prices stay as they are.
