## Machine Tempo 1.1

Cheese presses and looms join in.

- Cheese presses make cheese and goat cheese faster, looms weave cloth faster.
- They use the same speed setting as kegs and preserves jars: at 1.5 (default) cheese takes about 2 hours 15 minutes instead of 3 hours 20 minutes, cloth about 2 hours 40 minutes instead of 4. At 2.0 both take half the time.
- Your speed setting carries over. Inputs, outputs, quality and prices stay as they are, and the loom's chance of double cloth from quality wool is untouched.
