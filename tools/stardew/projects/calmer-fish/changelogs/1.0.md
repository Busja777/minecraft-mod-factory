## Calmer Fish 1.0

First release.

- Every non-legendary fish you catch with a rod is calmer in the fishing minigame: 51 fish, from anchovy to blobfish.
- Choose the level in the config: Light (difficulty -15 %), Medium (-30 %, default) or Strong (-45 %). No fish gets easier than a carp.
- Behaviour, seasons, times, weather, locations and sizes stay as they are. Legendary fish are unchanged.
