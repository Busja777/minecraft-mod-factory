## Calmer Fish 1.1

Legendary fish, if you want.

- New config option `Legendaries` (off by default): turn it on and the five legendary fish and their Legendary Fish II versions fight 15 % less, whatever level you picked. Legend drops from 110 to 94, Glacierfish from 100 to 85.
- With the option off, nothing changes: legendary fish keep their full challenge, all other fish work as in 1.0.
- Behaviour, seasons and locations of the legendary fish stay as they are.
