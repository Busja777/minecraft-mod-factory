## Honest Sell Prices 1.0

First release.

- Choose the sell price of three groups in the config, each on its own: crops, animal products and artisan goods. Every group has 75 %, 100 % (default, the normal game), 125 % and 150 %.
- Crops: 39 harvests from the fields, from parsnips to sweet gem berries. Wine, juice, jelly, pickles, dried fruit and flower honey take their price from the crop, so they follow this setting.
- Animal products: 17 kinds of eggs, milk, wool, duck feathers, rabbit's feet and truffles.
- Artisan goods: 13 goods with a fixed price, such as cheese, mayonnaise, cloth, beer, pale ale, mead, truffle oil and caviar. At 150 %, beer stops at 285g so it never sells for as much as the 400g Gus asks for it.
- The new price counts for the shipping bin and for selling in shops. Profession bonuses and quality still apply on top.
- Oil, vinegar, coffee and cactus fruit keep their normal price, because shops sell them or what they are made of.
- Carrot, summer squash, broccoli and powdermelon (new in 1.6) keep their normal price for now.
