## Fair Traveling Cart 1.0

First release.

- The traveling cart's regular stock costs at most a multiple of each item's sell price: choose 2x, 3x (default) or 4x in the config.
- At 3x, Coal (sells for 15g) costs 45g instead of 100g to 1,000g, and Starfruit (sells for 750g) costs 2,250g instead of up to 3,750g.
- At 4x the cart still rolls its own prices and keeps them whenever they are lower than the cap.
- What the cart sells, how much and on which days stays as it is. Furniture, Rare Seeds, Coffee Beans and the rare special items keep their prices.
