## Fair Villager Trades 1.0

First release.

- Everything villagers sell costs about a third fewer emeralds (rounded, never below one).
- Covers the armorer, cartographer, cleric, farmer, fisherman, fletcher, leather worker, librarian, shepherd, toolsmith and weaponsmith; butchers and masons already sell everything for one emerald.
- What villagers pay for your goods stays vanilla, as do trade levels, experience, uses and curing discounts.
- Enchanted books and the wandering trader keep their vanilla prices for now.
