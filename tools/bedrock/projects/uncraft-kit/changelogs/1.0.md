## Uncraft Kit 1.0

First release.

- Wool of all 16 colours gives 4 string.
- Quartz block, quartz pillar and chiseled quartz block give 4 nether quartz.
- Blocks of clay, snow, glowstone, amethyst, bricks, nether bricks, honeycomb, resin bricks and chiseled resin bricks give back their 4 pieces.
- Blue ice gives 9 packed ice, packed ice gives 9 ice, a dripstone block gives 4 pointed dripstone: 31 recipes.
- Every amount is what the cheapest vanilla recipe needs for the block, so no loop creates items.
