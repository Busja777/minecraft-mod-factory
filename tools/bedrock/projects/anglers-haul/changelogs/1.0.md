## Anglers Haul 1.0

First release.

- Fishing pulls up treasure more often: 8% of all catches instead of 5%.
- Less junk: 7% of all catches instead of 10%. Fish stay at 85%.
- Works the same in jungles, which keep their own fish and junk.
- Luck of the Sea still adds treasure and removes junk on top: at level III a cast gives about 14% treasure and 1% junk.
