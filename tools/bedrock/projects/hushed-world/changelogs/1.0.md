## Hushed World 1.0

First release.

- Villagers: idle chatter, haggling and the yes / no sounds at 40 % volume.
- Cows and mooshrooms: mooing at 40 % volume, for both cow sound variants.
- Nether portals: the hum of a lit portal at 35 % volume.
- Rain at 50 % volume.
- Every other sound, including hurt and death sounds and thunder, is unchanged.
