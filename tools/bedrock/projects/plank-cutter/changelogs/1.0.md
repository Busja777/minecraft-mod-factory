## Plank Cutter 1.0

First release.

- Cut planks into stairs, slabs, fences, fence gates, doors, trapdoors and pressure plates in the stonecutter.
- Works for all twelve woods: oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, bamboo, crimson and warped planks: 84 recipes.
- One plank gives two slabs or one of everything else.
