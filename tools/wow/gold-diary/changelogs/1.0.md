## Gold Diary 1.0

First release.

- Tracks every change to your gold and sorts it by source: loot, quests, vendor, repairs, Auction House, mail, trainer, flight paths, trade, barber & transmog and more.
- Window with three views: this session, today and the last 7 days.
- Shows earned, spent and net gold, plus your net gold per hour for the session.
- Gold you move into or out of the Warband bank or a guild bank is listed on its own and does not count as earned or spent.
- Open it with `/gd` or `/golddiary`. `/gd reset` starts a new session.
