## Flight Minutes 1.1

- New: hover a destination on the flight map to see how long the flight from where you stand takes, for example "Flight time: 2:41".
- Destinations you can fly to but have not timed yet say "not timed yet".
- Works on both flight maps of the game.
- As in 1.0, the times are shared by all your characters of the same faction, so a new character sees them from the first visit.
- Your routes from 1.0 are kept and used as they are.
