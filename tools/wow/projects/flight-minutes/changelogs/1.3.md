## Flight Minutes 1.3

- New **flight totals**: Flight Minutes adds up how many flights you took and how long you spent in the air, for this session and for all time on each character.
- `/fm stats` shows both totals, for example "This session: 3 flights, 7m 12s in the air."
- `/fm` now shows the totals under the number of known routes, and hovering the flight frame during a flight shows them too.
- `/fm stats reset` clears the all-time total of the character you are playing. Your routes and the session total stay.
- A session starts when you log in; a `/reload` keeps it going. Flights you stop early count for the totals, but still teach no route time.
- Your routes, sound choice and frame position from 1.2 are kept. The totals start at zero with this version.
