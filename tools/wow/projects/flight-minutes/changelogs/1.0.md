## Flight Minutes 1.0

First release.

- Times every flight path you take and remembers how long each route lasts. It ships without any data: every time is measured on your own flights.
- From the second flight on a route, a small frame shows your destination and a countdown until you land.
- On a new route the frame shows the time flown so far, and after landing the measured time is printed in chat.
- Keeps an average of the last five flights per route. Alliance and Horde routes are stored apart.
- Drag the frame to move it, the position is saved.
- `/fm` or `/flightminutes` shows how many routes are known, `/fm list` lists them with their times, `/fm forget` clears all routes.
