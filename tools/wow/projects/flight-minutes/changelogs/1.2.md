## Flight Minutes 1.2

- New **arrival sound**: Flight Minutes can play a short sound 10 seconds before the learned landing time, when you land, or both. It is off until you switch it on, so nothing changes unless you want it.
- `/fm sound before`, `/fm sound landing`, `/fm sound both` and `/fm sound off` set when it plays. `/fm sound` shows the setting.
- Pick the sound with `/fm sound bell`, `chime`, `horn`, `warning` or `whisper`. The new choice plays once so you can hear it.
- The early sound needs a timed route and does not play when you stop a flight early. Routes shorter than 20 seconds only get the landing sound.
- The sound plays on the Master channel, so you hear it even with sound effects muted.
- Your routes and the frame position from 1.1 are kept.
