## Flight Minutes 1.4

- Flight Minutes now speaks **German**: the flight frame, the line on the flight map, the chat messages and the totals. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- Flight point names and your faction keep coming from the game, so they show in your client's language as before.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/fm stats` or `/fm sound both`. The sound names `bell`, `chime`, `horn`, `warning` and `whisper` stay as they are, too.
- Your routes, sound choice, totals and frame position from 1.3 are kept.
