## Reset Clock 1.0

First release.

- `/resetclock` shows how long it is until the daily and the weekly reset.
- `/resetclock timer 10m tea` starts a timer with an optional label. Lengths like `90s`, `10m`, `1h30m` or a plain number of minutes, up to 24 hours.
- When the timer runs out you get a line in chat, a raid warning on screen and a sound.
- A running timer keeps going through `/reload` and logouts. `/resetclock timer` shows the time left, `/resetclock cancel` stops it.
- `/resetclock window` opens a small window with both countdowns and the timer, updated every second. Drag it anywhere, its position is remembered.
