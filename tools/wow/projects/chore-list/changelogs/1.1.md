## Chore List 1.1

- Chore List now speaks **German**: the window, the lines at the daily and weekly reset, the list in chat and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The window's headings Daily and Weekly come from the game itself, so they read exactly like in your client's language. In English they stay the same words.
- Your tasks stay exactly as you typed them.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/chores add daily Feed the pet` or `/chores done weekly 2`.
- Your lists, ticks and the window position from 1.0 are kept.
