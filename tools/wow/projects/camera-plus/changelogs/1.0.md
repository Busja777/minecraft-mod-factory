## Camera Plus 1.0

First release.

- `/cplus far` lets the camera zoom out up to the farthest distance the game allows (factor 2.6, the game's own setting `cameraDistanceMaxZoomFactor`). `/cplus far 2.2` picks a smaller step, values outside 1 to 2.6 are kept inside.
- `/cplus off` puts back the distance you had before, or the game's default.
- The setting is kept for your account and set again on every login.
- In combat the change waits until the fight is over.
- If you move the game's own camera distance slider while Camera Plus is on, Camera Plus switches off and keeps your choice.
- Three camera positions per character: `/cplus save 1` (to 3) saves the current camera, `/cplus 1` brings it back, `/cplus forget 1` empties a slot. They use the game's views 2 to 4, so the first-person view 1 and the game's view 5 stay untouched.
- `/cplus` or `/cameraplus` shows the current state and all commands.
