## Camera Plus 1.1

- Camera Plus now speaks **German**: the status, the help and every answer to a command, including the notes about combat and saved camera positions. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/cplus far 2.2` or `/cplus save 1`. As before, a decimal comma works too: `/cplus far 2,2`.
- Your camera distance setting, the value from before it and your saved camera positions from 1.0 are kept.
