## Quiet Please 1.1

- Quiet Please now speaks **German**: the list, the help and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The three sound options that `/quiet errors`, `/quiet emotes` and `/quiet pets` switch are named exactly like in the game's own sound settings, in your client's language. In English they now read Error Speech, Emote Sounds and Enable Pet Sounds.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/quiet add 12345` or `/quiet pets off`.
- Your list of muted sounds, their labels and your on/off setting from 1.0 are kept.
