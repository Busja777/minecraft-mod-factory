## Durability Watch 1.2

- New **repair cost history**: every visit to a vendor who can repair while your gear is damaged is noted with the date and the price of a full repair, for each character on its own.
- It also notes how you paid: with your own gold, with guild money, or not repaired at that visit. Repairing single items with the repair hammer counts as your own gold.
- `/dw history` lists your last 20 visits, newest first, and what your repairs cost in the last 7 days, with the part the guild paid. `/dw history clear` empties the list of the character you are playing.
- The tooltip of the display now shows the repair costs of the last 7 days.
- Opening the same vendor again a few minutes later at the same price is not counted twice. Durability Watch still never repairs by itself.
- Your settings from 1.0 and 1.1 are kept.
