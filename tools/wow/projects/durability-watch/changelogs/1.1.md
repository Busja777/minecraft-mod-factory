## Durability Watch 1.1

- New **warning before dungeons and raids**: when you enter one and an item is at 50% durability or less, you get one clear message on screen and in chat, with the instance and the most worn item, telling you to repair first.
- It warns only once per instance: not again after a wipe and the run back, and not after `/reload`. After a repair, the next visit warns again if your gear is worn.
- `/dw instance 40` sets your own level (5 to 90), `/dw instance off` switches the warning off, `/dw instance on` back on.
- `/dw` now also shows the instance warning setting. Your settings from 1.0 are kept.
