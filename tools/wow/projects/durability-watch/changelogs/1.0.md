## Durability Watch 1.0

First release.

- Shows the condition of your equipped gear in percent, overall and for the most worn item.
- Small display in green, yellow (50% or less) and red (20% or less). Drag it anywhere, its position is remembered. `/dw lock` locks it in place.
- Mouse over the display to see every damaged item with its slot.
- Warns you once, on screen and in chat, when an item drops to the warning level (25% by default). The next warning comes after a repair.
- At a vendor that can repair, tells you what a full repair costs. It never repairs by itself.
- `/dw` shows the state and the commands. `/dw warn 30` sets the warning level (5 to 90), `/dw hide` and `/dw show` hide or show the display.
