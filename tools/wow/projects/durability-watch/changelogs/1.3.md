## Durability Watch 1.3

- Durability Watch now speaks **German**. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- Slot names come from the game itself now, so they read exactly like on your character sheet, in your client's language.
- Dates in `/dw history` follow your client's language.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/dw history` or `/dw instance off`.
- Your settings and your repair cost history from 1.2 are kept.
