## Link Catcher 1.1

- Link Catcher now speaks **German**: the copy box, its hint and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The hint names the keys as on a German keyboard: Strg+C to copy, Esc to close.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/lc copy 50` or `/lc off`.
- Your on/off setting from 1.0 is kept.
