## Link Catcher 1.0

First release.

- Web addresses in chat become clickable: addresses with https or http, addresses starting with www. and short ones without www. that end in a common domain such as .com, .net, .org, .io or .gg.
- Click a link and a small box opens with the address already selected. Press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac) to copy it, Escape closes the box.
- Item links, colours and icons in a message stay as they are. Numbers like 1.5 and e-mail addresses are not turned into links.
- Works in say, yell, emote, whispers, Battle.net whispers, party, raid, instance, guild, officer, channels and communities.
- `/lc copy` shows the last 100 lines of your main chat window as plain text to copy, `/lc copy 50` the last 50 (up to 500).
- `/lc off` and `/lc on` switch the clickable links off and on. `/lc` or `/linkcatcher` lists all commands.
- Messages the game keeps hidden inside instances are passed on untouched and left out of the copy.
