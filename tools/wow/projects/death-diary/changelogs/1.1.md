## Death Diary 1.1

- Death Diary now speaks **German**: the note after a death, the summary, the list of your last deaths and the window. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The window's column headings Zone and Level and the row for deaths without a level come from the game itself, so they read exactly like in your client's language. In English they stay the same words.
- Dates follow your client's language.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/dd last` or `/dd reset confirm`.
- The diaries of all your characters, your quiet setting and the window position from 1.0 are kept.
