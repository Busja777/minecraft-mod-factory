## Death Diary 1.0

First release.

- Notes each of your deaths with zone, sub-zone, character level and time.
- `/dd` or `/deathdiary` shows how often this character has died, your top five zones and your deaths per level bracket (1-10, 11-20 and so on).
- `/dd last` lists your last 10 deaths, newest first.
- `/dd window` opens a small window with deaths per zone, per level bracket and your last death. Drag it anywhere, its position is remembered.
- One chat line after each death. `/dd quiet on` switches it off, `/dd quiet off` brings it back.
- `/dd reset confirm` clears the diary of the character you are playing. Other characters keep theirs.
- Every character has its own diary, the newest 500 deaths are kept.
