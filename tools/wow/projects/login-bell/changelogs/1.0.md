## Login Bell 1.0

First release.

- Rings a short bell and writes a clear line in chat when a friend or guild member comes online: "Friend online: Name" or "Guild member online: Name". The name can be clicked like any player name.
- Works with character friends, guild members and Battle.net friends. Friends who only log in to the mobile app are left out.
- Reads the game's own online message, so it works in every client language.
- The same person rings at most once every 5 minutes, so a reconnect does not ring again. When several people come online at once, each gets a line but the sound plays once.
- Stays quiet for the first 15 seconds after login or a reload, while the game fills the friend and guild lists.
- `/lb sound`, `/lb friends` and `/lb guild` switch each part on or off, `/lb test` plays the bell. `/lb` (or `/loginbell`) shows the settings. Settings are shared by all your characters.
