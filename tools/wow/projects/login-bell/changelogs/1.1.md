## Login Bell 1.1

- Login Bell now speaks **German**: the chat line when someone comes online ("Freund online: Name", "Gildenmitglied online: Name"), the settings, the help and the bell test. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- As in 1.0, the online message in chat is read in your client's own wording, so on a German client "ist jetzt online" counts just like "has come online" in English.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/lb sound off` or `/lb guild on`.
- Your settings from 1.0 are kept.
