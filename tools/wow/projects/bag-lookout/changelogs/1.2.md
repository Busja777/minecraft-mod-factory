## Bag Lookout 1.2

- New **choice of warning sound**: `/bl sound bell`, `chime`, `horn`, `warning` or `whisper` picks the sound and plays it once. `warning` is the sound of 1.0 and 1.1 and stays the default.
- `/bl sound none` or `/bl sound off` keeps the warnings silent, `/bl sound on` brings your chosen sound back. `/bl sound` lists the choices.
- New **warn once per session**: with `/bl warn once`, "almost full" and "full" each come only once until you log in again. `/bl warn always` warns every time the bags fill up again, as before.
- `/bl` now shows the chosen sound and how often you are warned.
- The warning threshold works as before: `/bl threshold <0-50>`.
- All settings from 1.1 are kept.
