## Bag Lookout 1.4

- Bag Lookout now speaks **German**: the counter, the warnings, the tooltip and every chat line. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The split view names each kind of bag exactly as the game does, in your client's language, for example "Herb Bag 12/36" or "Reagent Bag 30/36". A kind the game has no name for still shows as "Profession Bag".
- Amounts in `/bl cheap` and under the "bags full" warning now use the game's own coin display.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/bl view split` or `/bl warn once`. The sound names `warning`, `bell`, `chime`, `horn` and `whisper` stay as they are, too.
- All settings from 1.3 are kept.
