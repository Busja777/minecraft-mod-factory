## Bag Lookout 1.1

- New **split view by bag type**: under the counter, one line for the reagent bag and one line for each kind of profession bag, for example "Herbs 12/36" or "Mining 0/24". Several bags of the same kind are added up.
- Profession bags are named for herbs, mining, enchanting, engineering, gems, inscription, leather, fishing and cooking; any other kind is shown as "Profession".
- Each line turns yellow at your warning threshold and red when that bag type is full.
- Click the counter or type `/bl view split` and `/bl view total` to switch. The total view shows your normal bags, as in 1.0.
- Warnings work as before and only follow your normal bags.
- If you had the reagent bag shown in 1.0, you start in the split view. All other settings are kept.
