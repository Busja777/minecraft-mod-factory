## Bag Lookout 1.3

- New **cheapest items list**: `/bl cheap` shows the 5 items in your normal bags that are worth the least at a vendor, counting the whole stack in the slot. It tells you what to throw out or sell first when space runs short.
- Only grey, white and green items are listed. Quest items, items without a vendor price and anything blue or better are left out.
- Items in the reagent bag and in profession bags are not listed, because the warnings follow your normal bags.
- The "bags full" warning now has a second line with the cheapest item, for example "Cheapest: [Linen Cloth] (12c)".
- Items the game has not loaded yet are skipped, and `/bl cheap` says so. Try again a moment later.
- All settings from 1.2 are kept.
