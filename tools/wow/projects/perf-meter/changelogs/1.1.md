## Perf Meter 1.1

- Perf Meter now speaks **German**: the framerate on the meter, the hints in the tooltip and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- As in 1.0, the tooltip lines for framerate, latency and addon memory are the game's own, so they read the way your client writes them.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/pm lock` or `/pm reset`.
- The meter's place, the lock and whether it is shown from 1.0 are kept.
