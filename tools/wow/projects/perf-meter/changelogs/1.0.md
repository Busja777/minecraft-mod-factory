## Perf Meter 1.0

First release.

- A small movable line on screen shows your framerate, your home and world latency and the memory all your addons use together.
- Each value is green, yellow or red: framerate green from 45 fps and red below 25, latency green below 100 ms and red from 250 ms, addon memory green below 200 MB and red from 500 MB.
- Hover over the meter to see the same lines as the game's own performance tooltip, plus the five addons that use the most memory.
- Framerate and latency update once per second. Addon memory is measured every 15 seconds and never during combat, because measuring it is expensive.
- Drag the meter where you like and lock it. Its place, the lock and whether it is shown are saved for your whole account.
- `/perfmeter` or `/pm` shows or hides the meter, `/pm lock` and `/pm unlock` fix or free it, `/pm reset` puts it back at the top of the screen.
