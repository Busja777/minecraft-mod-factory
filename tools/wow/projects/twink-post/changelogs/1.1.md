## Twink Post 1.1

- Twink Post now speaks **German**: the tooltip of the mailbox button, the hint in the menu and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- As in 1.0, the level and the faction names come from your client, so a German client shows "Stufe 70" and its own faction names.
- Character and realm names stay exactly as the game writes them, so the recipient box gets the same `Name` or `Name-Realm` as before.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/twinkpost forget Name` or `/tp`.
- Your saved characters from 1.0 are kept.
