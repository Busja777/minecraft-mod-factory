## Twink Post 1.0

First release.

- Remembers every character of your account that logs in, with realm, class, level and faction. The list is shared by all characters of the account.
- On the Send Mail tab of the mailbox, a small button next to the recipient box opens a menu of your own characters. A click writes the name into the recipient box. Nothing is sent until you press Send yourself.
- The menu shows your characters on this realm and its connected realms, your own faction first, each name in its class colour with its level. A character on a connected realm is filled in as Name-Realm, the way the game expects it.
- The character you are playing is left out, and so is a neutral Pandaren that has not picked a faction yet.
- `/twinkpost` or `/tp` lists every character Twink Post knows. `/tp forget Name` (or `Name-Realm`) removes one, for example after a rename or a deleted character.
