## Loot Wishes 1.0

First release.

- Keep a wish list of items. Add one with `/lw add` and shift-click the item into the chat line, or type its item ID (`/lw add 19019`). An item link or a Wowhead address works too.
- When a wished item is in your loot window or comes up for a loot roll, a chat line and a short alert sound tell you.
- When you receive a wished item, or another player near you loots it, the chat says so. Items you got are marked on the list.
- When you open a merchant who sells a wished item, the chat names it, once per visit.
- The tooltip of a wished item shows "On your wish list".
- `/lw list` shows your list with how often and how long ago each item showed up, `/lw remove <item or number>` takes an item off, `/lw sound off` keeps the alerts silent.
- An item counts in every version: each difficulty and upgrade level of it is a hit.
- Shared by all characters of your account, up to 500 items.
- Open the help with `/lw` or `/lootwishes`.
