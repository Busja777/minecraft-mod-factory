## Loot Wishes 1.1

- Loot Wishes now speaks **German**: the alerts in chat, the wish list, the tooltip line and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- As in 1.0, loot lines in chat are read in your client's own wording, so on a German client "Ihr erhaltet Beute" counts just like "You receive loot" in English.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/lw add <item>` or `/lw sound off`.
- Your wish list, its hits and your sound setting from 1.0 are kept.
