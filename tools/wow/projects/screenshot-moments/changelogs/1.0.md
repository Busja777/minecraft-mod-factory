## Screenshot Moments 1.0

First release.

- Takes a screenshot on its own when you reach a new level, earn an achievement or loot an item of Epic quality or better.
- The screenshot follows one second after the moment, so the game's level-up or achievement toast is in the picture.
- At most one screenshot every 10 seconds, and none during a loading screen. Moments in the same second share one screenshot.
- A chat line names the moment: the level, the achievement or the item.
- `/smo off` switches the screenshots off, `/smo on` brings them back. The setting counts for all your characters.
- `/smo test` takes a test screenshot. `/smo` or `/shotmoments` shows whether it is on and lists the commands.
- Screenshots land in the game's own Screenshots folder, like the ones you take by key.
