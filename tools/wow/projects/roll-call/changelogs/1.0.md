## Roll Call 1.0

First release.

- `/rollcall start` opens a roll call for 30 seconds, `/rollcall start 60` for any time from 5 to 120 seconds.
- A small window lists every `/roll` it sees, highest first, with the range of each roll and a countdown.
- Only the first roll of each player counts; later rolls of the same player are ignored and counted.
- Equal rolls are marked as a tie, rolls that are not 1-100 (like `/roll 1000`) are shown in orange.
- When the time is up the winner (or the tie) appears in your own chat window. Nothing is sent to other players.
- Reads the roll line in your client's language, using the game's own text.
- `/rollcall stop` ends early, `/rollcall` (or `/rolls`) shows the last result, also after a reload.
- Where the game hides chat text from addons (in instances), the window says that rolls may be missing.
- The window can be moved and remembers its place; Escape closes it.
