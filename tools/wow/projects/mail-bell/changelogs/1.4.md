## Mail Bell 1.4

- Mail Bell now speaks **German**: the new mail line, the expiry warning, the login lines and `/mb letters`. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- Senders, subjects and character names keep coming from the game, so they show exactly as in your mailbox.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/mb letters` or `/mb expire off`. The bell names `bell`, `chime`, `horn`, `warning` and `whisper` stay as they are, too.
- Your characters, letters and settings from 1.3 are kept.
