## Mail Bell 1.0

First release.

- Rings a bell and prints one chat line when new mail arrives. The sound plays on the Master channel, so you hear it even with sound effects muted.
- Mail that was already there when you log in does not ring, and the bell rings at most once every 30 seconds.
- Remembers which of your characters have unread mail. A character counts as done once you have emptied its mailbox or closed it with no new mail left.
- A few seconds after login, one chat line names your other characters with mail waiting.
- `/mb` or `/mailbell` shows that list and the settings. `/mb sound on|off` and `/mb login on|off` switch the bell and the login list, `/mb forget Name-Realm` removes a character.
