## Mail Bell 1.2

- New **choice of bell**: `/mb sound bell`, `chime`, `horn`, `warning` or `whisper` picks the sound for new mail and plays it once so you can hear it.
- `bell` is the sound of 1.0 and 1.1 and stays the default. If your game does not know a sound, Mail Bell rings the old bell instead.
- `/mb sound on/off` works as before and keeps your choice. Picking a bell switches the sound on.
- `/mb sound` and `/mb` show the chosen bell.
- Your characters and settings from 1.1 are kept.
