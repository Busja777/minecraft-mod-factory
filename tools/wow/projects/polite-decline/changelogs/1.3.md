## Polite Decline 1.3

- Polite Decline now speaks **German**. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- On a German client the default reply whisper is German too, since the stranger most likely plays on a German realm. A reply you set yourself with `/pd whisper text` is sent exactly as you typed it.
- Dates in `/pd log` follow your client's language.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/pd allow Name` or `/pd whisper on`.
- Your settings, allow list, reply whisper and log from 1.2 are kept.
