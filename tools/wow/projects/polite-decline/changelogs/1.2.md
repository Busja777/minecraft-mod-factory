## Polite Decline 1.2

- New **log**: every duel and group invite that Polite Decline turns down is noted with the time, the name and the kind of request, also in quiet mode.
- `/pd log` lists what was declined this session, newest first, with the number of declines so far.
- `/pd log all` lists the last 50 declined requests across all your sessions and characters, so you can see who kept asking while you were busy.
- `/pd log clear` empties the log.
- Requests that got through (friends, guild mates, your allow list, anyone the addon was unsure about) are not logged, because they were not declined.
- `/pd` now mentions `/pd log` in its help. Your settings, allow list and reply whisper from 1.0 and 1.1 are kept.
