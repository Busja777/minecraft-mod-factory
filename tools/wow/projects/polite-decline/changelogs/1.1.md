## Polite Decline 1.1

- New **reply whisper**: a stranger whose duel or group invite was declined can get one short, polite whisper, so they know it was not personal. It is off until you switch it on with `/pd whisper on`.
- The default reply is "Thanks, but I don't take duels or group invites from people I don't know. Have a good day!" `/pd whisper text` followed by your own words sets your own (up to 200 characters), `/pd whisper reset` brings back the default, `/pd whisper` shows the reply.
- No spam: the same character gets the whisper at most once every 30 minutes, and two whispers never go out within 10 seconds.
- No whispers inside dungeons, raids, battlegrounds, arenas or scenarios, or while the game has chat locked. Declining works there as before.
- Friends, guild mates, your allow list and anyone the addon is unsure about are never whispered, because they are never declined.
- Your settings and allow list from 1.0 are kept.

- The help text now reads "on/off" instead of "on|off", which the chat could show garbled.
