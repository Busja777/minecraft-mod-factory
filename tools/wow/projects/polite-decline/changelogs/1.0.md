## Polite Decline 1.0

First release.

- Turns down duel requests and group invitations from strangers and hides their popups.
- Friends, Battle.net friends and guild mates always get through, and so does everyone on your own allow list.
- When it cannot tell who someone is, it lets the request through rather than decline it by mistake.
- One chat line per declined request with the name and a count for this session. `/pd quiet on` turns the lines off.
- `/pd` or `/politedecline` shows the settings. `/pd duels on|off` and `/pd invites on|off` switch each half, `/pd allow Name`, `/pd unallow Name` and `/pd list` keep the allow list.
