## Junk Butler 1.0

First release.

- Sells every grey item that has a vendor price as soon as you open a vendor, a few items at a time.
- Repairs all your gear at vendors that can repair, paid from your own gold. If your gold is short, it tries once more after the sale.
- One chat line tells you how many items were sold, for how much, and what the repair cost.
- Hold Shift while you open a vendor to skip both for that visit.
- `/jb` or `/junkbutler` shows the settings. `/jb sell` and `/jb repair` switch each half on or off.
