## Junk Butler 1.3

- New **sale log**: every vendor visit that sells something is noted with the date, the vendor, the items, how many and what they brought in. Each character keeps its last 20 visits.
- `/jb log` lists the visits, newest first, with the total of each visit and of all of them together.
- `/jb log` followed by a number shows what was sold in that visit, for example `/jb log 1` for the latest one. Items show as links you can click.
- Visits from the last 15 minutes are marked with how long ago they were. Sold something by mistake? The vendor's Buyback tab may still have it, but the game keeps only your latest sold items there and empties it when you log out.
- `/jb log clear` empties the log of the character you are playing.
- Your settings, keep list and guild bank choice from 1.2 stay as they are.
