## Junk Butler 1.2

- New: **guild bank repairs**. Type `/jb guild on` and Junk Butler repairs from your guild bank first, when your guild rank may repair from it and the bank (and your daily limit) covers the cost.
- When the guild bank cannot pay, your own gold pays, just like before. If the guild bank turns a repair down, Junk Butler notices and pays from your gold instead.
- The chat line tells you when the guild bank paid.
- `/jb guild off` switches it off again. It starts switched off, so nothing changes until you turn it on.
- `/jb` now also shows the guild bank setting. Your settings and keep list from 1.1 stay as they are.
