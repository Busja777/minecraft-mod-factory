## Junk Butler 1.4

- Junk Butler now speaks **German**. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The hint in `/jb log` names the vendor's Buyback tab exactly as your vendor window does, in your client's language.
- Dates in `/jb log` follow your client's language.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/jb keep` or `/jb guild on`.
- Your settings, keep list, guild bank choice and sale log from 1.3 stay as they are.
