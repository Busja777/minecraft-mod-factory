## Gather Tally 1.1

- Gather Tally now speaks **German**: the small display, its tooltip and every chat line. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- Item names and links come from the game itself, in your client's language, as before.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/gt reset` or `/gt hide`.
- Your display position and your hide or show setting from 1.0 are kept.
