## Gather Tally 1.0

First release.

- Counts every herb and ore you loot this session, from the loot window (autoloot included).
- Shows herbs and ores per hour, measured from the first herb or ore of the session. The rate appears after 5 minutes.
- Lists the items you gathered most, with their counts.
- Small display that appears with your first herb or ore. Drag it anywhere, its position is remembered. Mouse over it to see the top items.
- `/gt` (or `/gathertally`) prints the session in chat, `/gt reset` starts a new session, `/gt hide` and `/gt show` hide or show the display.
