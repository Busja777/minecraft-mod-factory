## Zone Notes 1.1

- Zone Notes now speaks **German**: the notes in chat, the window and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- Zone names come from the game itself, so they read exactly like in your client's language.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/zn add <text>` or `/zn quiet on`.
- Your notes, your popup and quiet settings and the window position from 1.0 are kept.
