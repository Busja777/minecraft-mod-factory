## Zone Notes 1.0

First release.

- Write your own notes for the zone you are in with `/zn add <text>`, up to 10 notes per zone and 200 characters per note.
- When you enter a zone with notes, they show in chat and in a small window. Drag the window anywhere, its position is remembered. Close it with its close button, a right-click or Escape.
- `/zn` or `/zonenotes` shows the notes of this zone and all commands. `/zn list` shows the notes, `/zn remove <number>` deletes one, `/zn all` lists every zone with notes.
- `/zn window` opens or closes the window. `/zn popup off` keeps it closed when you enter a zone, `/zn quiet on` turns off the chat lines.
- Notes are shared by all characters of your account. Small maps such as caves and buildings count as the zone around them.
