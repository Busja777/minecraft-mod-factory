## Fishing Log 1.1

- Fishing Log now speaks **German**: the session, the catches per zone and every chat line. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- A catch in a place without a zone name is listed under the game's own word for "Unknown", in your client's language. In English it stays "Unknown".
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/fl zones` or `/fl reset`.
- Your catches per zone from 1.0 are kept.
