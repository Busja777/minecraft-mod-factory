## Fishing Log 1.0

First release.

- Counts every catch: each loot window from fishing is one catch, together with the number of items in it.
- Keeps your catches per zone, for the current session and for all time.
- Shows your catches per hour for the session, once you have two catches and five minutes of fishing.
- Type `/fl` (or `/fishinglog`) for the session in chat, `/fl zones` for all your catches per zone, `/fl reset` to start a new session.
