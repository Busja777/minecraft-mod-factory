## Level Clock 1.2

- New **window options**: `/lc lock` keeps the window in place so you cannot drag it by accident, `/lc unlock` lets you move it again.
- `/lc scale` followed by a number from 0.8 to 1.5 (or 80 to 150) makes the window smaller or larger. It stays where you put it.
- The window now remembers whether you left it open and opens again at your next login.
- `/lc hidemax` keeps the window closed on characters at maximum level, and closes it when you reach the maximum level. Your other characters still see it, and `/lc` still opens it whenever you want.
- `/lc options` shows these settings. All of them are saved for your whole account.
- Your sessions and window position from 1.1 stay as they are, and the History view works as before.
