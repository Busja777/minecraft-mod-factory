## Level Clock 1.3

- New **level history** for every character: from now on Level Clock notes when you reach each level.
- New command `/lc levels`: lists the last 10 levels of the character you are playing, newest first, with the date and how long each level took.
- When the game has told your played time in this session (type `/played` once, or another addon asks for it), each level also gets its /played time, and the time it took is counted in played time. Otherwise it shows the real time between two level-ups, marked as "real time", because that includes time spent offline.
- Typing `/played` right after a level-up still fills in when that level was reached.
- Your sessions, history and window settings from 1.2 stay as they are. Levels reached before 1.3 are not known, so the list starts with your next level-up.
