## Level Clock 1.0

First release.

- Shows your XP per hour for the current session, counted correctly across level-ups.
- Estimates the time until your next level and shows the XP you still need.
- Shows your rested bonus as a percentage of the level.
- Small window with session XP and session time. Drag it anywhere, its position is remembered.
- At maximum level the window says so instead of showing numbers.
- Open it with `/lc` or `/levelclock`. `/lc reset` starts a new session.
