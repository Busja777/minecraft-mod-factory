## Level Clock 1.1

- New **History** view: click the History button in the window to see your last 10 sessions with XP for this character: when they started, how long they lasted, XP gained, XP per hour and levels gained.
- New command `/lc history`: lists the same sessions in the chat.
- A UI reload or a quick relog (within five minutes) continues the session instead of splitting it. `/lc reset` still starts a fresh one.
- The last session saved by version 1.0 becomes the first entry of the history, nothing is lost.
