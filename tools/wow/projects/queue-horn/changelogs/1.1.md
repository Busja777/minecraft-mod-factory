## Queue Horn 1.1

- New: **choose the sound for each alert**. Five sounds of the game to pick from: horn, warning, alarm, invite and whisper.
- `/qh sound` lists the sound of every alert and the choices. `/qh sound lfg alarm` gives the Group Finder alert the alarm and plays it once so you hear it. The same works for `ready`, `pvp` and `pet`.
- `/qh test lfg` (or `ready`, `pvp`, `pet`) plays the full test alert with the sound chosen for it. `/qh test` on its own works as before.
- Every alert keeps the horn until you choose another sound, so nothing changes unless you want it to. Your settings from 1.0 are kept.
