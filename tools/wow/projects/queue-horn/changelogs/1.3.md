## Queue Horn 1.3

- Queue Horn now speaks **German**. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The alerts are named the way the game itself names them, in your client's language: Group Finder, Ready Check, Battleground and Arena, Pet Battle. On English clients they are now written with capital letters, for example "Ready Check" instead of "Ready check".
- The hint about the game's "Sound in Background" option names that option exactly as your sound settings do.
- The commands stay the same in every language, and so do the sound and colour names you type, for example `/qh sound lfg alarm` or `/qh flash ready red`.
- Your settings, sounds and screen flash colours from 1.2 are kept.
