## Emote Bar 1.0

First release.

- A small bar with your favourite emotes as buttons. One click runs the emote, at your target if you have one.
- Starts with six friendly emotes: wave, hello, thank, cheer, dance and laugh.
- `/emotebar add <emote>` adds a button, up to 12. Type the emote the way you type it in chat, with or without the slash: `/emotebar add bow` or `/emotebar add /hi`.
- Only emotes the game itself knows are accepted. On a client in another language its own emote commands work too, and the English ones still do.
- `/emotebar remove <emote or number>` removes a button, by its emote or its place on the bar. `/emotebar list` lists them with their numbers.
- Drag the bar anywhere; `/emotebar lock` locks or unlocks it. `/emotebar` shows or hides it.
- Your emotes, the position and the lock are saved for your whole account.
- The bar works in and out of combat. If the game refuses an emote at a moment, the bar says so in chat.
- `/eb` is the short form.
