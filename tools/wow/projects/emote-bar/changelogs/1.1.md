## Emote Bar 1.1

- Emote Bar now speaks **German**: the tooltips, the hints on the bar and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- As in 1.0, the buttons show your client's own emote commands, so on a German client they read /winken or /hallo, and the English /wave or /hello still work when you add or remove an emote.
- The examples in the German help use the German emote commands, for example `/emotebar add winken`.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/emotebar add <emote>` or `/eb lock`.
- Your emotes, the lock and the bar's position from 1.0 are kept.
