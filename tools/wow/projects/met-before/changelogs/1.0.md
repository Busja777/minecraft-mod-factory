## Met Before 1.0

First release.

- Remembers every player you share a party or raid with: when you first and last met, how often you grouped and the zone of your last meeting.
- A meeting counts once per group, not on every change of the group. A reload inside the group does not count it again.
- The tooltip of a player you met shows "Met before: 3 times, last 2 days ago" and your note on them.
- `/mb note Name-Realm text` sets your note (no text removes it), `/mb note target text` notes your current target.
- `/mb list` shows the last 10 players you grouped with, `/mb forget Name-Realm` removes a player.
- The realm can be left out for players on your own realm or with a unique name.
- Shared by all characters of your account, the 2000 players met most recently are kept, players with a note last.
- Inside instances the game can hide player names from addons; those players are skipped there.
- Open the help with `/mb` or `/metbefore`.
