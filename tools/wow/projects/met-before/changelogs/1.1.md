## Met Before 1.1

- Met Before now speaks **German**: the tooltip lines, the list of players and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The word Note in front of your note in the tooltip comes from the game itself, so it reads exactly like in your client's language. In English it stays the same word.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/metbefore note target` or `/metbefore forget Name-Realm`.
- The players you met, your notes and the current group from 1.0 are kept.
