## Alt Almanac 1.1

- New **Played** column: the total time played of every character, like `/played`, shown as minutes, hours or days.
- The total line now also adds up the played time of all your characters.
- The time is read quietly a few seconds after login, without the usual `/played` lines in your chat. Your own `/played` still prints as always.
- The character you are playing keeps counting while the window is open.
- Characters you have not logged in with since this update show a dash and are not in the total yet. Log in with each of them once.
- The window is a little wider so the new column fits. All your saved characters from 1.0 are kept.
