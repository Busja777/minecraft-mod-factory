## Alt Almanac 1.0

First release.

- One window with all your characters: name in class colour, level, gold, zone and rested bonus.
- Each character is updated whenever you play it: on login, when your gold, level, zone or rested bonus changes, and on logout.
- The rested bonus of characters you are not playing keeps growing in the list, faster for those who logged out in an inn or a city. Resting characters are shown in blue.
- Shows when you last played each character, and the total gold of all of them.
- Sorted by level, then by name. Up to 15 rows, the last one counts any further characters.
- Open it with `/aa` or `/altalmanac`. `/aa forget Name-Realm` removes a character from the list. Drag the window anywhere, its position is remembered.
