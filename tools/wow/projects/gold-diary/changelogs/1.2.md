## Gold Diary 1.2

- New **History** view: the last 13 days, one row per day with gold earned and spent.
- Shows your net gold over those days, the average per active day and your best day.
- Gold Diary now has an entry in the addon menu next to the minimap: click it to open the diary, hover it to see today's net gold.
- The window is a little wider so long gold amounts fit.
