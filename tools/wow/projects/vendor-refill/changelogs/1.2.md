## Vendor Refill 1.2

- New: **refill from the bank**. When you open your bank, items of your list move from the bank into your bags until you have the count you want. Your character bank and your Warband bank both count. So the next vendor only buys what the bank did not have.
- Only items on your list are moved, and never more than the count you want. Whole stacks move as they are, bigger stacks are split.
- One chat line tells you what arrived in your bags, for example "Taken from the bank: Water x12." Another one tells you when your bags were full.
- Nothing is moved in combat or while you hold an item on the cursor. Hold Shift while you open the bank to skip one visit.
- `/vr bank off` switches only the bank part off, `/vr bank on` back on, `/vr bank` shows the setting. It is on from the start. `/vr off` switches all refilling off, at vendors and at the bank.
- Your list and settings from 1.1 are kept.
