## Vendor Refill 1.0

First release.

- Keep a list of consumables with the count you want in your bags. Each character has its own list.
- At every vendor that sells an item of your list, it buys just enough to reach that count, a few purchases at a time.
- Items the vendor sells in bundles are bought in whole bundles. Items that cost a currency other than gold and sold out items are skipped.
- One chat line tells you what was bought and what it cost. A second line tells you when your gold was not enough for everything.
- Hold Shift while you open a vendor to skip one visit.
- `/vr add <item> <count>`, `/vr remove <item>` and `/vr list` manage the list (item link or item ID). `/vr on` and `/vr off` switch refilling. `/vr` or `/vendorrefill` shows the state and the commands.
