## Vendor Refill 1.1

- New **low stock warning**: when an item of your list drops below 25 % of the count you want, one chat line tells you, for example "Water is running low: 4 of 20 left." So you know to stock up before you reach the next vendor.
- Each item warns once. It warns again only after you restocked it.
- No warnings while a vendor window is open, and the warning keeps working when refilling is switched off.
- `/vr warn <percent>` sets the share (1 to 90), `/vr warn off` switches the warning off, `/vr warn` shows the setting.
- Your list and settings from 1.0 are kept.
