## Quest Autopilot 1.2

- New switch `/qa gossip`: when a character has no quests for you and offers exactly one line of conversation, the autopilot picks it for you. Handy for long talks that are only "Tell me more" page after page.
- Only plain conversation lines are picked. Shops, trainers, banks, flight maps, innkeepers, options with rewards or spells and every real choice between two or more lines stay yours.
- If a line asks you to confirm something (gold, a skip, a trip), the game's question is left to you and the autopilot never picks that line again, also after a restart.
- The same line is never picked twice in a row, and at most five lines per conversation.
- Holding Shift and `/qa off` stop it, like everything else. The switch starts off, so nothing changes until you type `/qa gossip`.
- `/qa` now also shows the new switch. Your settings and manual quests from 1.1 stay as they are.
