## Quest Autopilot 1.0

First release.

- Accepts a quest as soon as a quest giver offers it.
- Hands in finished quests and takes the reward when there is nothing to choose.
- Works through quest givers with several quests: finished ones first, then new ones.
- Stops when a quest offers several rewards and tells you in chat, so you pick yourself.
- Never hands in a quest that asks for gold.
- Repeatable quests are handed in once but never taken again on their own, so your items are safe.
- Hold Shift while you talk to someone to pause it for that conversation.
- `/qa` or `/questautopilot` shows the state. `/qa on` and `/qa off` switch everything, `/qa accept` and `/qa turnin` switch one half.
