## Quest Autopilot 1.1

- New: keep chosen quests **manual**. The autopilot does not accept, hand in or pick them at a quest giver; everything else keeps running.
- `/qa manual` keeps the quest you just saw manual, even if the autopilot has already taken it. `/qa manual` followed by a quest ID, a quest name or a shift-clicked quest link adds one by hand.
- `/qa auto` followed by an ID or name hands a quest back to the autopilot, `/qa list` shows your list.
- New switch `/qa dailies`: leaves all daily and weekly quests to you. It is off, so nothing changes until you type it.
- `/qa` now also shows the daily switch and how many quests you keep manual.
- Your settings from 1.0 stay as they are.
