## Quest Autopilot 1.3

- New **reward hint**: when a quest lets you choose a reward, the one a vendor pays the most for gets a small coin in the reward window, and one chat line names it with its price, for example "Highest vendor value: [Staff] (1g 20s)".
- A stack counts with all its items. The hint only shows when the game knows every price; if a price is still loading, it appears as soon as it arrives. When nothing is worth anything or two rewards share the top price, there is no hint.
- It is only about the vendor price. It does not compare gear, and the autopilot still never picks a reward for you.
- `/qa hint on` and `/qa hint off` switch it; it starts on. Holding Shift and `/qa off` stop it, like everything else.
- `/qa` now also shows the hint switch. Your settings, manual quests and dialogue choices from 1.2 stay as they are.
