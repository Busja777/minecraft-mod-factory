## Quest Autopilot 1.4

- Quest Autopilot now speaks **German**. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The price in the reward hint uses the game's own coin letters in your client's language, for example "1G 20S 3K" on a German client. English clients still see "1g 20s 3c".
- Quest names and reward links come from the game as before, in your client's language.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/qa manual` or `/qa hint off`.
- Your settings, manual quests and dialogue choices from 1.3 stay as they are.
