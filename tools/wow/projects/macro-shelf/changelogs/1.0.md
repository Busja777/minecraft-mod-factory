## Macro Shelf 1.0

First release.

- `/macroshelf backup` saves your account macros and the macros of the character you are playing. Each character keeps its own backup.
- A place without any macros at that moment never replaces a backup that holds some.
- `/macroshelf restore` creates the macros from the backup that are missing. Existing macros are never changed or deleted.
- `/macroshelf export` shows all your macros as text, already selected, so Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac) copies them.
- `/macroshelf import` opens a box to paste such a text into; "Create macros" creates them, as account or character macros like in the text.
- Text with any mistake creates nothing at all and lists the mistakes with their line numbers. Names have up to 16 characters, macro texts up to 255.
- A macro whose name already exists is skipped and named in chat: rename one of them to keep both. The identical macro counts as already there.
- Only as many macros are created as there are free slots; the rest is named in chat.
- Creating macros is refused in combat.
- `/mshelf` is the short form, `/macroshelf help` lists all commands.
