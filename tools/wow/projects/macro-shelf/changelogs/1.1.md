## Macro Shelf 1.1

- Macro Shelf now speaks **German**: the window, its buttons and hints, the reports after a backup, restore or import, the mistakes found in a pasted text and every answer to a command. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- The window and its buttons are a little wider, so the longer German texts fit. In English it looks the same, only with more room.
- Backup dates follow your client's language.
- The export text keeps its words (`[macro]`, `account`, `character`, `[end]`) in every language, so an export from a German client imports on an English one and back.
- The commands stay the same in every language, for example `/macroshelf backup` or `/mshelf import`.
- Your backups from 1.0 are kept.
