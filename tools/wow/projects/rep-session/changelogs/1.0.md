## Rep Session 1.0

First release.

- Shows the reputation you gained this session with the faction on your reputation bar.
- Shows your reputation per hour and estimates the time until your next standing, once you have played five minutes.
- Works with regular factions, renown factions and friendship factions, counted correctly across a new standing or renown level.
- Switch the watched faction as often as you like: each faction keeps its own count for the session.
- At the highest standing it says max instead of showing a time.
- `/rs` or `/repsession` prints it to chat, `/rs show` opens a small window, `/rs reset` starts a new session.
