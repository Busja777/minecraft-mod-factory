## Chat Calc 1.0

First release.

- `/calc` followed by a sum works it out right in your chat window: `+ - * / % ^` and parentheses, with the usual order (powers first, then `* / %`, then `+ -`). `x` works as "times" as well.
- Understands gold: `12g 50s 3c`, `1250000c`, `1.5g` or `2 gold 5 silver`. A result in gold is shown as gold, silver and copper with the game's coin icons, plus the amount in gold and in copper.
- Sensible gold rules: `12g 50s * 3` and `100g / 4` stay gold, `100g / 12g 50s` gives a plain number, `10g % 3` shows the copper left over when 10 gold is split three ways.
- Numbers may have decimals (`1.5`) and thousands separators (`1,250,000`).
- Clear messages instead of Lua errors: division by zero, unbalanced parentheses, a missing number or operator, gold mixed with plain numbers, input over 200 characters and numbers that are too large.
- Results go only to your own chat window, nothing is sent to other players.
- `/calc` or `/chatcalc` on their own show a short help.
