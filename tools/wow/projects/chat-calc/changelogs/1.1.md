## Chat Calc 1.1

- Chat Calc now speaks **German**: the help and every message about what went wrong in a sum. On clients in other languages it stays in English.
- Answers and messages use your client's own coin letters: on a German client the amount in gold and in copper reads `(37.5G, 375,000K)`, with K for Kupfer, next to the game's coin icons. On an English client nothing changes: `g`, `s` and `c` as before.
- Numbers are written as before in every language: the decimal point is `.` (`1.5`), the comma only separates thousands (`1,250,000`). The German help and the message for `1,5` say so.
- Typing stays the same in every language: `/calc 12g 50s * 3` works everywhere, and on a German client `3k` for copper works as in 1.0.
